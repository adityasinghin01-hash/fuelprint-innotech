## Composition and material language

- One continuous landscape and one persistent Three.js canvas. Never separate unrelated scene cards.
- Opening title: **FUELPRINT**, heavy locally bundled Manrope, real accessible DOM text.
- HQ is the central architectural landmark: layered ivory roof, teal glazing, launch terrace, clear
  nameplate, arrival steps, rooftop sensor and communications mast.
- Six fictional factories use three different authored roof forms. Only S01–S06 identify factories.
- Sage/forest roofs, cream ceramic chimneys, green slate roads, organic trees and continuous meadow.
- Soft world-space cloud volumes and looping illustrative chimney smoke. No diagnostic HUD or game UI.
- Labels stay readable; UI controls use Phosphor icons and at least 44 px touch targets.

## Scientific and ownership boundaries

fuel, guilt or legal proof. No readings, outcomes or probabilities are calculated by this scene.

review remain human decisions. The approval beat is narrative text, never an operational command.
